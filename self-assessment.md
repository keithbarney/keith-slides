# Self-Assessment — Keith Barney

> "I want to make beautiful things, even if nobody cares." — Saul Bass

---

## Strengths

- **Systems thinker** — I see how pieces connect across platforms and teams. Example: Added a Design QA column to our Jira board that loops work between dev and design until approved—reduced follow-up tickets significantly.
- **Technical fluency** — I speak developer language. Tokens, components, versioning, build pipelines. I can collaborate effectively and call BS when needed.
- **Business-minded** — Startup background keeps me focused on customer value. Ship, learn, iterate—perfection comes over time.
- **Disagree without drama** — I can have honest disagreements without getting emotional. Took years to develop, but it enables better outcomes.

---

## Weaknesses

- **Too direct** — I've been told I come across as blunt.
  - *Example:* I used to post screenshots of pattern issues in Slack asking who owned it. A manager told my boss it made people feel called out. I now use DMs instead.
- **Presentation skills** — I can build a strong deck, but I'm more comfortable with a script than winging it. Actively working on this.
- **Scale gap** — Haven't managed a DS across multiple brands or large verticals yet. Confident I can figure it out, but haven't done it.
