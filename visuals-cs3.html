CS3 Problem — Visual Options

Chart-style progress over time — migration from legacy to Atlas. Light, not visually heavy.

Option 1

Rising Line

A single dotted curve drawing itself left to right, rising upward. Simple adoption-over-time. The line builds dot by dot and loops.

Option 2

Crossover

Legacy lines fall while the Atlas line rises. The crossover moment is the story — the point where the new system overtakes the old.

Option 3

Stepped Bars

Vertical dot columns that grow taller period by period, each one a quarter of progress. Clean bar chart energy with the dot aesthetic.

Option 4

Area Fill

A curve that draws and fills beneath itself with scattered dots. The filled area grows over time — cumulative progress made tangible.

Option 5

Staircase

A stepped line that climbs in discrete jumps — each step a migration milestone. Dots trace the path and linger at each plateau.
